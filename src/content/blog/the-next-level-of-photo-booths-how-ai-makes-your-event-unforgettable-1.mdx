### The role of AI in our lives

Artificial intelligence has made tremendous advancements in recent years. For example, AI-powered translation programs have broken down language barriers, while AI algorithms can create works of art that are nearly indistinguishable from those made by humans.

Photo and video technology is one field where AI has made particularly impressive progress. AI-powered cameras can automatically detect their surroundings and adjust settings to capture the best possible image. AI algorithms enable us to create ultra-high-resolution photos and videos, as well as remove unwanted objects from images.

### AI photo booth: An innovative solution for every event

When talking about AI in photography and videography, we cannot overlook the concept of the AI photo booth. Photo booths have been a popular way to capture memorable moments for years—whether at weddings, corporate events, or holiday parties.
