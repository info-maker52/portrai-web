### How does an AI photobooth enhance your company summer event?

1. **Fun and interactive entertainment** – The AI photobooth adds excitement to your summer days by letting employees capture moments with dynamic, fun, and imaginative themes. The AI creates personalized and creative photos, making each image one of a kind.

2. **Branded memories** – Companies can use the AI photobooth to add logos and brand elements to every photo. It’s a great way to showcase company values and strengthen team spirit.

3. **Instant sharing** – Employees can instantly download their photos, email them, or share them on social media. This boosts the event’s visibility and generates positive buzz beyond the company walls.

4. **Themed backgrounds and effects** – The AI photobooth lets you transform your summer day photos with custom backgrounds—whether it’s a palm-lined beach, a mountain view, or even a spaceship. Special effects and high-quality visuals turn every photo into a memorable piece of art.

5. **Lasting memories** – AI-generated photos aren’t just fleeting entertainment, they’re lasting memories for the whole team. Whether printed or shared digitally, the images serve as a joyful reminder of the event. You can choose individual portraits or group shots - whatever fits the vibe.

### Where to place the AI photobooth during summer events?

- **Near the stage or main area** – to capture the liveliest moments where the crowd gathers most.

- **Close to activity zones** – so guests can snap photos during breaks between activities.

- **By the entrance** – to let everyone take a welcome photo as they arrive—or one last snap before they leave.
